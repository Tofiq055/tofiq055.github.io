Since the magazine was designed for children, the target audience of the article was also set as children.

Playing games is highly enjoyable; but have you ever thought that you could make a game yourself one day?

If you were to ask what one of the common pleasures of children and adults is, I would say games. Especially video games! These games, on which most of us spend hours, are not just tools for having fun; they are also something you can create yourself. Yes, game development! The game industry in Turkey has been rising rapidly in recent years. For example, there are many companies like "Peak Games", a mobile game company sold for a whopping 1.8 billion dollars in June 2020, or "TaleWorlds Entertainment", the creator of the world-famous "Mount and Blade" series. 

However, is it possible to make a game without being a company? No matter how challenging today's conditions are, there are people who develop games on their own and become successful. Like Eric Barone, the creator of "Stardew Valley", or Markus "Notch" Persson, the creator of "Minecraft", which most of us play with joy. The common point of these people is their passion for making a game that appeals to their own tastes. 

But this process can be as challenging as it is fun. For example, the development of GTA V took a full 5 years and a team of 1000 people. After the game's release, updates were made for years. So, what do you think is the reason for the difficulty of making games? What do we encounter when playing games? Worlds to explore, enemies to defeat, points to collect... There are many different areas like these to deal with. But where should one start? With an idea! You need the idea of what kind of game you will make, the inspiration to strike, and to decide on certain things in your head. This process can happen in many different ways.

## The Legend Born from a Cave: Shigeru Miyamoto and Zelda

Now let's meet a game designer named Shigeru Miyamoto. This friend, who loved exploring the forests in his village when he was little, found a dark and mysterious cave while wandering one day and wondered what was inside. What kind of creatures, dungeons, treasures, and guards protecting them were in there?

This limitless imagination inspired him to make games as he grew up. The Legend of Zelda transformed from an idea full of a child's dreams into a game series that gained worldwide success and fame over time. It has succeeded in being a game series that is still being made, played, and loved since 1994.

## From Idea to Drawing: The Birth of Characters and Worlds

The idea phase of a game is only a beginning; there are many things to think about before starting production. For example: how many levels will there be in the game, will there be enemies, and if so, what will they look like? Game designers find, write, and share the answers to such questions with concept artists. 

These friends love drawing and decide what the game will look like. Some draw the characters, while others design the world where the characters will live. You might notice that these drawings do not look much like the characters we see in the final games. Because these drawings are not yet the final state of the characters. To bring them to their final form, the drawings are given to 3D modelers. These friends design the characters in three dimensions on computers, just like in the game. Sometimes it can take weeks to complete a single character.

## The Magic of Code: The World of Developers

However, having characters appear in the game does not mean they are playable yet; someone needs to "bring them to life." These people are the engineer friends we refer to as software developers. 

Even though it might look like they are writing random letters and numbers at first glance, they are actually writing what we call code, which tells the computer what to do. These codes determine how the character should react when the player presses a button, how they will fight an enemy, and how they will respond to potential situations. Because there are thousands of such possibilities, thousands of lines of code are written. Consequently, bugs (errors) can occur.

## Bug Hunters: The Heroes of Games "Testers"

While these bugs can sometimes make the game look funny, most of the time they break it and make it unplayable. This is very frustrating for players, so game developers do not want players to experience this. Before we encounter these bugs, someone needs to play the game, discover those errors, and report them to the developers. 

We call these people "Testers." These friends get paid to play games! While it might seem like a very fun and easy job at first glance, believe me, it is not that simple. Because they do not play the games just for fun; they play to discover all possible bugs. When they find an error—something that shouldn't be in the game—they take notes and share those parts with the developers.

## Word-of-Mouth Success: Ad-Free Minecraft

After all these stages are finished, the marketing phase of the game remains, which is another area of expertise. Marketing is an important way to share the game with the world and ensure people discover it. Video trailers and cover designs are used to create ad campaigns and shared with people on social media platforms like YouTube. 

But sometimes, though rarely, if a game is very fun, it can spread by word of mouth and become popular even without an advertising campaign. For instance, Minecraft is a highly successful game sold over 200 million times, yet zero money was spent on its advertising. Reaching so many people entirely through the community's love and support, this game is a true success story.

## Start Your Own Adventure! 🚀

And that's how it is, dear friends... Making games can be a truly magical adventure. Starting from an idea, using your imagination, and with a little bit of your effort, you too can make your own game. Maybe one day, you will create a globally successful game and make your country proud.

Do not forget that big game developers were also once little dreamers like you, and they achieved great success by following their passion for making games. Follow your own dreams, get inspired about making games, and strive to show yourself to the world. Who knows, maybe the most popular games of the future will come from your hands!
